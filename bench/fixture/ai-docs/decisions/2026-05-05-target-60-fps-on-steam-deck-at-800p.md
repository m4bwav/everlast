# Target 60 fps on Steam Deck at 800p

## Context
The Steam Deck is the main handheld target; frame time spikes made combat feel uneven.

## Decision
A 16.6 ms frame budget at 1280x800 with medium settings.

## Reasons
Verified status needs a stable frame rate.

## Rejected alternatives
- 40 fps at higher settings: uneven input.

## Consequences
Effects over budget get a handheld variant.
