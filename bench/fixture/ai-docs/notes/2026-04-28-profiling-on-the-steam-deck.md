# Profiling on the Steam Deck

## Summary
How to attach the Unity profiler to a development build running on the Steam Deck.

## Details
Enable Autoconnect Profiler, open the port in the firewall on the handheld, connect over the local network by IP. Deep profiling is too slow there; use markers.
